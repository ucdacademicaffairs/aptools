How Entitlements Are Calculated
  • PDLL: Calendar days include start and end dates. Weeks = total calendar days ÷ 7. Workdays and remaining entitlement are not calculated.
  • FMLA: Calendar days and workdays are counted. Remaining entitlement = 84 − total calendar days.
  • CFRA: Calendar days and workdays are counted. Remaining entitlement = 84 − total calendar days.
  • PFCB: Workdays are counted. Remaining entitlement = 56 − total workdays. Calendar days and weeks are not calculated.

Weeks are displayed to two decimal places. Remaining entitlement follows the subtraction above and can be negative.
