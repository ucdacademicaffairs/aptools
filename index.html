Mini-Apps

Summer Compensation Calculator

Derives a maximum available summer compensation from an annual salary. Users can then explore summer-research scenarios by activating months and adjusting FTE. Built for faculty on 9/12 ("academic year") appointments (for now), and optimized for inclusion in SiteFarm via iFrame.

Last updated: October 23rd, 2025

Added teaching calculations.

Launch Tool →

Sabbatical Planning Calculator

Enter a sabbatical credit total and annual salary, then activate quarters, play with salary level and residency, and see the impact on sabbatical credit usage and total salary. Optimized for inclusion in SiteFarm via iFrame.

Last updated: July 23rd, 2025

Trimmed UI to better fit iFrame.

Launch Tool →

Roster Parser

Upload a roster export from SIS (XLS, XLSX, CSV) to return a simple list of "course code / course number / instructor name (or blank) / CRN". Handles "untrusted" and "trusted" exports.

Last updated: August 28th, 2025

Added a hybrid XLS handler.

Launch Tool →

IPA-Disclosure Double Checker

This tool takes a Support Assignments XLSX exported from IPA and quickly pulls out name, email, and title. Its purpose is to streamline the process of double-checking whether or not an employee has completed the Disclosure Process.

Last updated: September 2nd, 2025

First version.

Launch Tool →

Student Employment Cost Calculator

Create an "employment schedule" for graduate student employees and display the costs associated. Features proper proration of same-quarter multiple-appointment benefits and work-study.

Last updated: August 28th, 2025

Further improvements to work-study handling (50% GSR w/ 1-unit no longer has an error).
