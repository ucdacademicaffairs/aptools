IPA-Disclosure Double Checker

How to Use

  1. Export the desired ASE list from the IPA tool as an XLSX file.
  2. Click 'Choose File' and select your downloaded IPA export in this tool.
  3. A preview of the extracted Name, Email, and Appointment Title will appear below.
  4. Click 'Export as XLSX' to download the formatted results.
  5. Use the 'Email Template' section below to prepare your email to L&S.
