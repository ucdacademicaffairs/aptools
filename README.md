The intention of these little tools is to (a) provide administrative staff working in the academic personnel and graduate education spaces with easily-referenced calculators to aid in faculty and student advising, while (b) also making the same tools those staff use accessible to faculty in a "non-actionable" form, so that they are able to play with live scenarios and to some extent self-advise. 

## [summercomp.html](https://blueclustertools.ucdavis.edu/summercomp.html)
This little calculator allows users to enter an annual salary from which point it derives a maximum available summer compensation. Users can then play with different summer-research compensation scenarios by activating the different months and playing with the FTE within. It is currently built solely for faculty on 9/12 ("academic year" appointments), and would need revision before it has any utility for those on 11/12 ("fiscal") appoinments (let alone faculty split between the two). It is optimized for inclusion in SiteFarm pages via iFrame. 

Last updated: July 23rd, 2025 (Trimmed UI to better fit iFrame.)

## [sabbatical.html](https://blueclustertools.ucdavis.edu/sabbatical.html)
This calculator allows users to enter a sabbatical credit total and an annual salary, from which point they can activate sabbatical for Fall, Winter, and Spring Quarters, play with salary level and residency, and see the impact on their sabbatical credit usage and total salary. It is optimized for inclusion in SiteFarm pages via iFrame. 

Last updated: July 23rd, 2025 (Trimmed UI to better fit iFrame.)

## [studentemployment.html](https://blueclustertools.ucdavis.edu/studentemployment.html)
This calculator allows users to create an "employment schedule" for graduate student employees and display the costs associated. It is agnostic on the utility of including NRST costs for ASEs (as opposed to GSRs), and simply warns users if they go near NRST. Proper proration of same-quarter multiple-appointment benefits is in, as is work-study, although I'm continuing to test out each.

Last updated: July 29th, 2025 (Further improvements to work-study handling.)

***[studentemployment-facultygsr.html](https://blueclustertools.ucdavis.edu/studentemployment-facultygsr.html)***

***[studentemployment-facultystdt.html](https://blueclustertools.ucdavis.edu/studentemployment-facultystdt.html)***

Simplified versions of the studentemployment.html calculator optimized for faculty "messing-around" use. They are very restrictive, single-quarter, and include a warning that the estimate is just that. There's one for GSRs and one for STDTs, optimized for inclusion in SiteFarm pages via iFrame. 

Last updated: August 11th, 2025 (First version.)
