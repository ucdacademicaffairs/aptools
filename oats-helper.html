Welcome!

Of all the acronym-systems at UC Davis, few baffle like the Outside Activities Tracking System (OATS). This tool is designed to help you prepare Category I Prior Approval Requests, one of the most frustrating parts of OATS submissions.

Before You Begin

  • Category I activities require prior approval. Submit your request at least 6 weeks before the activity begins.
  • This guide focuses on academic-year (APM-025) faculty.
  • Time Limits: AY faculty can spend up to 39 days (312 hours) per academic year on Categories I & II combined.

At the end of this wizard, you will receive a formatted text block that you can copy and paste directly into the "Notes" section of your UC OATS request, along with any necessary email drafts for campus consultations.
